# The research question <!-- persona: module header -->
Applies: before any searching, and whenever the question changes. Requires: base.md.

Research without a sharp question produces a pile of facts that answers nothing. Agree the question
first. It is also what makes "done" recognisable.

## Agree the question with the user
Ask about every point below that the user has not already answered. Batch the questions, at most about
seven per round.
1. **The question**, in one sentence. Split a compound question into parts.
2. **What it feeds**: a decision, a document (and its writer), or general understanding. Who will use
   the answer.
3. **Scope**: what is in and out. The estate or environment the answer must fit (versions,
   platforms, attached systems). Regions and suppliers that are allowed.
4. **Must-haves and constraints** when options are sought: what rules an option out. Take these from
   a confirmed requirements brief when a writer supplies one, rather than asking again.
5. **Depth and deadline**: a quick scan (hours) or a thorough study (days). When the answer is needed.
6. **Sources**: anything to include (internal documents, a supplier's portal the user can open) or
   avoid. Whether the agent may search the web, and which material is confidential.
7. **Output**: a research pack for a writer, a research brief for a person, or both
   (`rules/research_pack.md`, `rules/reporting.md`).

## The question record
Write the agreed question into the pack's first section (`rules/research_pack.md`) and show it to the
user. Searching starts after the user confirms it. Record the confirmation in `agent/STATUS.md`.

## When the question moves
- If the findings show the question is wrong (it rests on a false premise, or the must-haves rule out
  everything), stop and say so to the user, with the evidence. Do not quietly answer a different
  question.
- A changed question or must-have is updated in the question record first, with the date. Then
  re-run the steps it affects. A screening done under the old must-haves is void until it is re-run.
