# Comparing a shortlist <!-- persona: module header -->
Applies: when the research question asks to compare products, editions, vendors or approaches.
Requires: base.md, `rules/method.md`.

Building and screening the longlist is covered in `rules/method.md`. This module covers comparing
the shortlisted options fairly.

## Criteria
- Must-haves are a screen, applied before comparing (`rules/method.md`). They are not scored.
- Compare only on what separates the shortlisted options, taken from the question's should-haves and
  constraints. Keep criteria countable ("supports PFC per queue", not "good storage support") and few,
  usually three to six.
- Weights come from the user or the writer's brief. If none were given, compare without scores and say
  so. Do not invent weights.

## Compare like for like
Same edition, same deployment model, same unit, same price basis (list or quoted, with or without
support, first year or over the horizon). When options differ, say so in a table note.

## Fairness
- Describe every option at the same depth and from the same kind of source. Do not describe one from
  its datasheet and another from a forum thread.
- For each option, state when it would be the right choice. If an option wins on nothing, it does not
  belong on the shortlist.
- Name what would change the outcome: an open decision, a price, a missing fact.

## Output
- In the pack: a table of options against criteria, with source ids per cell, plus a few sentences per
  option. `unknown` is a legal cell value. A guess is not.
- Product and vendor names as registered. No made-up benchmarks. Never put numbers from two different
  test setups in one row.
- Every comparison states "valid as of <date>".
